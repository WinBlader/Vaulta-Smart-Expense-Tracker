Designed for Daily Clarity

Everything You Need to Master Your Money

Clean, fast, and purpose-built features crafted to make personal budgeting a seamless daily habit.

Effortless Expense & Income Logging

Log transactions in seconds with dedicated income and expense layouts. Customize payee notes with custom titles, categorize spending (Groceries, Utilities, Salary, Rent), and backdate entries with an intuitive calendar picker.

Smart Filters & Custom Date Ranges

Find any transaction with real-time keyword search across notes, categories, and amounts. Quickly isolate Income or Expense, and filter by Today, This Week, This Month, or custom date intervals with an integrated date range picker.

Direct PDF Downloads & Sharing

Download official, branded transaction receipts and comprehensive financial statement PDFs directly into your device's Downloads/Vaulta folder. Easily share PNG receipt cards or PDF files with your accountant or family.

Visual Spending Analytics

Gain instant financial insights with smooth visual spending curves, category progress distribution bars, and cash flow velocity charts. Set monthly budget ceilings and receive alerts before you overspend.

Flexible Sign-In & Cloud Sync

Sign in using your Email, Google One-Tap, or Mobile Number with an editable side-by-side country code supporting all global dialing codes (+1, +44, +91, +971, etc.). Or use Vaulta 100% anonymously in Guest Mode.

Offline-First & Account Isolation

Your financial ledger stays responsive with or without an active internet connection. Strict multi-user data isolation ensures that when you log out or switch accounts, previous transactions are never accessible to another user.

Privacy & Trust

Your Money. Your Data. Zero Compromises.

We believe financial tracking should be strictly private, secure, and under your control.

Offline-First by Design

Your transactions and budgets are stored securely right on your Android device. You can track your finances without ever creating an account.

We Never Sell Your Data

Vaulta does not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal or financial information with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Encrypted Cloud Sync

If you choose to sign in, your account profile is securely synchronized with strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies.

Account & Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You can wipe local data anytime by logging out, or request complete account and cloud deletion.
